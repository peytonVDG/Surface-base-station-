---
title: Chicken Tortilla Soup
servings: 6
time: 45 min
rating: 4.5
tags: [soup, chicken, weeknight]
---
## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 pound boneless chicken breast
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 1 cup corn
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 limes, juiced
- tortilla strips, avocado and cheese to serve

## Steps
1. Warm the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft, then stir in the garlic, cumin and chili powder for 1 minute.
2. Add the broth, tomatoes and chicken. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently for 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
3. Lift the chicken out onto a plate and shred it with two forks.
4. Return the chicken to the pot with the beans and corn. Simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Stir in the cilantro and lime juice. Taste and add salt.
6. Ladle into bowls and top with tortilla strips, avocado and cheese.
