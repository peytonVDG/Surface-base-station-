---
title: Banana Bread
servings: 10
time: 1 hr 15 min
rating: 4
tags: [bread, baking, breakfast]
---
## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 1/2 cups flour

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl, then stir in the butter.
3. Mix in the sugar, egg and vanilla. Sprinkle over the baking soda and salt, then stir in the flour until just combined.
4. Pour into the pan and bake for 1 hour. A toothpick should come out clean.
5. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes before slicing.
