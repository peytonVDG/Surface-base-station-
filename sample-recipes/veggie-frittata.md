---
title: Veggie Frittata
servings: 4
time: 25 min
rating: 4
tags: [eggs, breakfast, vegetarian, weeknight]
---
## Ingredients
- 8 eggs
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cups spinach
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Whisk the eggs, milk and salt.
2. Warm the oil in an oven-safe skillet. Cook the pepper for 4 minutes, then stir in the spinach until wilted.
3. Pour the eggs over the vegetables and scatter the cheese on top. Cook on the stove for 2 minutes.
4. Move the skillet to the oven and bake for 12 minutes, until set in the middle.
5. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
