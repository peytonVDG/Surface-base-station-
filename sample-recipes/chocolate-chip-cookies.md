---
title: Chocolate Chip Cookies
servings: 24
time: 30 min
rating: 5
tags: [dessert, cookies, baking]
---
## Ingredients
- 2 1/4 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Whisk the flour, baking soda and salt in a bowl.
2. Beat the butter and both sugars until fluffy, about 3 minutes. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
3. Mix in the flour, then fold in the chocolate chips.
4. Drop rounded spoonfuls onto baking sheets, 2 inches apart.
5. Bake for 10 minutes, until the edges are golden.
6. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then move to a rack.
