## Ingredients
- 2 pie crusts
- 6 cups sliced apples
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 egg, beaten

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Press one crust into a pie plate.
2. Toss the apples with the sugar, flour, cinnamon and lemon juice. Pile into the crust and dot with butter.
3. Cover with the second crust, crimp the edges, cut a few slits and brush with the egg.
4. Bake for 15 minutes, then lower the oven to 350°F.
5. Bake for 40 minutes more, until golden and bubbling. Cover the edges with foil if they brown too fast.
6. Cool for 2 hours so the filling sets.
