---
title: Buttermilk Pancakes
servings: 4
time: 20 min
rating: 4
tags: [breakfast, quick]
---
## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/4 cups buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 3 tablespoons melted butter

## Steps
1. Whisk the dry ingredients together. In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk, egg and butter.
2. Pour the wet into the dry and stir just until combined. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
3. Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat.
4. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook for 2 minutes, until bubbles form, then flip and cook for 1 minute more.
5. Serve warm with butter and syrup.
